# Minimal Sync Focus logo directions

The companion apps use one bold semantic mark in black and white: Sync Spend pairs a rupee with a rounded enclosure; Sync Tasks pairs a check with a circular ring. Sync Focus needs a similarly direct symbol for a focus timer.

- **Focus dial:** an open progress ring, minute hand, and central focus point. It carries the circular family shape and directly indicates timed focus.
- **Focus window:** four rounded focus brackets and a central point. It indicates concentration more directly, with less emphasis on time.

Both SVGs are original transparent vector concepts. They are exploratory and have not replaced the current app logo or launcher assets. Review them at small sizes and on both dark and light surfaces before choosing one.

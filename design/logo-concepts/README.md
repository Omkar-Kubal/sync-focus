## Prompt set

- **A:** “Logo concept for Sync Focus, a premium focus timer app. ONE simple vector-friendly icon centered on a pure white square canvas: four black rounded arc segments create an open focus aperture around one small central black dot, with subtle clock-like progression. Distinctive optical balance, readable at app icon and 24-pixel size. Flat black geometry only, ample padding. No text, no numerals, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup.”
- **B:** “Logo concept for Sync Focus, a premium focus timer app. Create ONE standalone icon mark centered on a pure white square canvas, generous empty padding. Direction B: a bold circular progress ring with a deliberate open gap at the upper right; inside the ring, two slim rounded vertical pause bars aligned with calm precision. The whole silhouette should express intentional focus and pausing distractions, distinct from a generic media pause button. Flat pure black vector-like geometry, balanced negative space, readable at 24 pixels and as a mobile app icon. No text, no numerals, no gradient, no shadow, no 3D, no mockup, no background shape.”
- **C:** “Logo concept for Sync Focus, a premium focus timer app. Create ONE standalone icon mark centered on a pure white square canvas with generous padding. Direction C: an abstract hourglass of attention made from two opposing bold rounded crescent strokes that narrow toward one small central focus point; the negative space should suggest time passing and mental clarity. Visually distinct from a standard clock, target, or media button. Geometric, confident, calm, optically balanced, readable at 24 pixels and as a mobile app icon. Flat pure black vector-like geometry only. No text, no numerals, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no background shape.”
